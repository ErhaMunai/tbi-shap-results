Study cohort eligibility

Adults with acute TBI during their first eligible admission. Their first ICU admission was non-elective and occurred within 24 hours of hospital admission or study-defined first hospital contact. They were alive in the same ICU at hour 24.

Both systolic blood pressure and SpO₂ had valid records in at least 12 of the first 24 hours, with at least two paired hours in each of the 0 to <6 h and 18 to <24 h windows.

The current inputs are based on a simulated example.

All eight predictors are required.
